BUGBREAK — UPDATE 2: STORMFRONT

NEW PERMANENT MODE
- STORMFRONT: 30 waves of rain, flooding, thunder and lightning hazards.
- Three Stormfront biomes: Rain-Soaked Lawn, Flooded Flower Bed, Tempest Porch.
- Eight Stormfront-exclusive enemy species.
- Flood Widow at Wave 10, Thunder Mantis at Wave 20, Tempest Monarch final at Wave 30.
- Six original Stormfront music tracks plus thunder SFX.

NEW PLAYABLE BUGS
- Bombardier Beetle — Chemical Artillery. Boiling Volley fires a caustic spread and burning pools.
- Stormglass Dragonfly — Tempest Champion. Beat Stormfront to unlock permanently. Thunder Lance teleports through a lightning line and chains damage.

NEW PROGRESSION
- Storm Shards are earned in Stormfront and saved permanently.
- Four permanent Storm Evolution upgrades: Conductive Shell, Rainrunner Legs, Cloudheart, Lightning Rod Nest.
- Two additional permanent upgrades: Storm Capacitor and Weathered Chitin.
- Six new storm mutations and four new storm relics.

ENEMY MODE
- Stormfront is fully supported in Enemy Mode.
- Storm-exclusive enemies and bosses have permanent Gem ownership, Enemy Level requirements and wave gates.
- Tempest Monarch is the Stormfront final enemy class.
- Owner enemy override still bypasses wave/ownership gates after Owner Access is unlocked.

OWNER / CREATOR
- Owner Access, Create Bug (30 saves), owner wave control and owner enemy override all remain.
- Owner tools include Storm Shards, Storm upgrades and Stormfront completion controls.

SAVE COMPATIBILITY
- The game still uses the same localStorage key: bugbreak-update1-save
- Existing Update 1 / 1.2.x saves migrate forward with Stormfront fields added safely.
